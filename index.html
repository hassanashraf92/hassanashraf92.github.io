AI Studio

A personal tool for editing and publishing my own videos.

AI Studio is a private, single-user application I run on my own computer. It helps me script, edit and caption videos for my own channels, and then upload those videos to my own accounts on YouTube and TikTok.

It is not a public service. There are no other users, no sign-up, and no hosted version. It connects to TikTok only through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, and only to the single TikTok account that I personally authorise.

What it does with TikTok

  • Reads my basic profile info (user.info.basic) so it can show which account is connected.
  • Uploads a video I created to my TikTok drafts (video.upload), where I finish and post it in the TikTok app.
  • Posts a video I created directly to my own profile (video.publish) when I choose that option.
