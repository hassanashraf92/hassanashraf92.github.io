Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Summary

AI Studio ("the App") is a private, single-user application run by Hassan Ashraf on personal hardware. It has no other users, no server, and no analytics. It stores data only on that machine. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

What the App does not do

  • It does not read, collect, or store data about any other TikTok user.
  • On Facebook and Instagram, the only data about other people it reads is the comments they leave on my own posts, as described above. It does not read anyone's profile, friends, or activity.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics services.
  • It does not send data to any server operated by me — there is no backend beyond my own computer.
  • It does not use TikTok data to train machine-learning models.

Third parties

Data travels only between my computer and the official APIs of the platforms I connect (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram). Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies, including the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and profile details are kept only while the account is connected. Disconnecting the account in the App deletes its stored tokens and revokes them with TikTok. Access can also be revoked at any time from TikTok's own settings (Profile → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the App's local database removes all stored data permanently.

For Facebook and Instagram, disconnecting an account in the App deletes its stored tokens; the grant is revoked with Meta once no other connected account relies on it. Access can also be removed at any time from Facebook (Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → Remove). To have any data the App stored about you deleted — for example a comment you left on one of my posts — email the address below and it will be deleted from the local database within 30 days.

Children

The App is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone other than its single operator.
